Chairman's Statement

"Nurturing Faith, Knowledge, & Economic Independence in Teso North"

Welcome to Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group. Founded in the heart of Teso North, Busia County, our mission is to rally young Muslims around the virtues of Islamic leadership, economic self-reliance, community service (*Usafi*), and agricultural production (*Agri-Youth*).

"The best of people are those who are most beneficial to others." — Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Sahihi Al-Jami')

Agricultural Self-Reliance

Cultivating food security through maize and cash crop farming in Teso North.

Youth Empowerment

Table banking, micro-investment, and skill building for economic resilience.

Food Security & Youth Jobs

Agri-Youth Farming Initiative

Empowering Teso North youth through modern maize and crop agriculture, turning idle land into thriving food production and youth income generation.

Feature: photo22.jpg

Sustainable Crop Farming in Action

Changara Muslim Youth working directly in the maize crop fields, cultivating staple food and learning agronomy skills.

Maize & Staple Grain Farming

We lease and cultivate arable land across Teso North Sub-County. Produce from maize harvests supports Madrasas, relief distribution, and group table-banking reserves.

Youth Skills & Agronomy

Training unemployed local youth in modern organic farming practices, crop protection, post-harvest handling, and agricultural business management.

Reinvestment into Table Banking

Profits from harvest sales feed directly into our Youth Table Banking fund, offering interest-free or low-cost micro-loans for youth startups.

Community Sanitation Drive

"Usafi & Environmental Service"

In Islam, cleanliness is half of faith (*An-Nadhafatu Minal Iman*). The Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group routinely organizes weekend cleanliness drives (*Usafi*) across mosques, primary schools, market centers, and public pathways in Teso North.
