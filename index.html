Sustainable Agriculture

Agri-Youth Crop Production

Transforming youth potential into agricultural productivity. We lease and cultivate farmland across Teso North, producing high-yield maize, beans, and fresh vegetables to strengthen food security and boost youth livelihoods.

Cleanliness is Faith

Usafi Drives & Mosque Maintenance Initiative

Inspired by Islamic teachings on hygiene and community service, our youth group regularly organizes voluntary sanitation campaigns ("Usafi") across Changara market center, local schools, and surrounding mosques.

Mosque Deep-Cleaning & Maintenance

Sanitizing prayer halls, maintaining ablution centers (Wudhu areas), and repairing carpets and surroundings.

Market Center Waste Cleanups

Clearing drainage systems, disposing of plastic waste, and promoting proper sanitation in Changara township.

Tree Planting & Environmental Greening

Planting shade trees and fruits around mosques and schools in Teso North to fight climate change.
