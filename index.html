Who We Are

Empowering Youth Across Teso North

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group (CMY) was established to foster unity, moral uprightness, socio-economic independence, and community service among young Muslims in Busia County.

1. Islamic Knowledge & Seminars

Organizing annual youth Islamic conventions, Quranic recitation contests, and moral mentorship with recognized scholars across Busia.

View Seminar Details

2. Youth Agribusiness & Farming

Commercial farming projects including maize, passion fruit, poultry, and vegetables to ensure youth self-reliance and food security.

Explore Agribusiness

3. Welfare & Table Banking

Shariah-compliant interest-free revolving micro-loans, emergency support for youth entrepreneurs, widows, and orphans.

4. Sanitation & Care

Monthly community sanitation drives, cleaning mosques, marketplaces, unblocking drainage, and tree planting across Changara.

Cleanliness in Action

Youth Community Sanitation & Environmental Care

"Cleanliness is half of faith (Tahara)." — Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) [Sahih Muslim]

The Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group believes faith must manifest in public service. Our environmental care program mobilizes dozens of energetic youth every month to perform critical sanitation duties across Teso North Sub-County.

Mosque Sanitation

Deep cleaning mosque grounds, ablution areas, and surrounding roads.

Market Cleanups

Clearing trash and unblocking drainage ditches in Changara markets.

Tree Planting Drives

Planting indigenous shade and fruit trees around community institutions.

Hygiene Education

Sensitizing community households on proper waste management.

Youth Agribusiness & Food Security
