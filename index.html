Who We Are

United in Faith and Community Progress

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group (CMY) is a dedicated community-based organization located in Changara, Teso North Sub-County, Busia County, Kenya.

We bring together Muslim youth and community members to foster spiritual growth, economic self-reliance, youth mentorship, and local development. Through group discussions, Madrasa support, local initiatives, and brotherhood, we strive to build a peaceful and prosperous future.

📍 Location:

Changara Division, Teso North Sub-County, Busia County, Kenya

Islamic Guidance & Youth Halaqas

Youth Empowerment & Self-Help

Supporting local Madrasas, mentoring younger children, organizing charitable outreaches, and promoting peaceful coexistence across Changara Division.
