Who We Are

Building a strong, unified, and self-reliant Muslim youth community.

🕌 Faith & Guidance

Spreading Islamic principles, moral values, and brotherhood to encourage righteousness and kindness across our local area.

🌱 Youth Empowerment

Creating social and economic opportunities, skill-building initiatives, and peer support to build self-reliance.

🤝 Community Support

Participating in welfare, charity, community service, and working hand-in-hand with residents in Teso North and Busia County.

"Jummah Mubarak & Community Unity"

"Spread goodness, strengthen brotherhood, and be a positive force in your community. Every small act of kindness is charity."

Our Focus Areas

How we serve our members and society.

Islamic Education & Reminders

Regular Halaqas, Jummah reminders, and sessions focused on character building and sound Islamic ethics.

Economic & Skill Initiatives

Promoting group savings, entrepreneurship awareness, and vocational development to combat youth unemployment.

Social Welfare & Outreach

Visiting the sick, supporting community programs, and fostering peaceful coexistence within Changara Division.
