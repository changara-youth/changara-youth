Who We Are

Dedicated to Islamic Faith, Unity & Youth Self-Reliance

Our Vision

To establish a vibrant, morally upright, and economically empowered Muslim youth community in Teso North Sub-County that leads positive social, environmental, and spiritual development.

Our Mission

To unite Muslim youth through educational seminars, sustainable agricultural projects, community sanitation drives, madrasa support, and direct charitable relief for orphans and needy families.

Key Core Values

Ihlas (Sincerity)
Ushirika (Unity)
Ukweli (Transparency)
Kujitegemea (Self-Reliance)

Economic Self-Reliance
