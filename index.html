Who We Are

United in Faith, Service & Community Progress

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group (CMY) is a community-based organization rooted in Changara, Teso North Sub-County, Busia County. Founded on the Islamic values of brotherhood, compassion, and self-reliance, our group brings together young Muslims to drive positive social and spiritual impact.

Spiritual Growth

Conducting youth seminars, Quranic study circles, and moral mentorship across local mosques.

Social Welfare

Supporting orphans, vulnerable households, and distributing food during Holy periods.

"The best of people are those who are most beneficial to others."

— Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him)

What We Do

Our Core Community Activities

Dedicated to uplifting Changara through faith-based initiatives, seasonal relief, and youth empowerment.

Year-Round

Community Welfare & Support

Supporting vulnerable households, widows, and orphans in Changara and across Teso North. We organize environmental cleanup drives, hospital visits, and hardship relief for families in need.

Support Community Drive
Holy Month

Ramadan Iftar & Eid Fitr

Hosting community Iftar gatherings at local mosques during Ramadan and distributing essential food baskets (rice, flour, sugar, oil) to needy families so everyone can celebrate Eid ul-Fitr with joy.

Sponsor Iftar Basket
Eid al-Adha

Qurbani Meat Distribution

Facilitating livestock sacrifice (Qurbani) during Eid al-Adha. Meat is packaged and distributed directly to impoverished families in Changara and neighboring villages across the sub-county.

Partner for Qurbani
Educational

Annual Youth Seminars

Educational workshops bringing together youth and Muslim scholars from Changara and surrounding mosques. Topics include Islamic character, career guidance, and drug abuse prevention.

See December Event
Self Help

Youth Savings & Skill Building

Promoting table banking, group investments, and vocational training to empower members financially and foster sustainable self-reliance among young people.

Join Self Help Group
Networking

Inter-Mosque Collaboration

Uniting youth groups from neighbouring mosques in Teso North and Busia County for joint sports tournaments, Islamic quizzes, and collaborative community projects.
