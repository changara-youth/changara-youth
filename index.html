Who We Are

Empowering Teso North Youth Through Faith & Action

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group (CMY) is a officially registered community-based youth initiative in Teso North Sub-County, Busia County. We focus on Islamic spiritual growth, youth agribusiness, economic table banking, and community welfare.

Islamic Knowledge & Quran

Organizing regional Quran recitation competitions, youth seminars, and inviting scholars to nurture moral values and religious literacy among young Muslims.

Quran Seminar & Competitions

Youth Agri-Business

Commercial maize farming, passion fruit orchards, and poultry rearing in Teso North to build economic independence and youth food security.

Commercial Yields

Welfare & Table Banking

Monthly revolving micro-loans, emergency support packs, Ramadan food distribution, and empowering members to launch micro-enterprises.

Financial Empowerment

Community Cleaning & Trees

Regular mosque and market sanitation drives, combined with youth tree planting initiatives across Busia County for environmental care.

Sanitation & Greening
