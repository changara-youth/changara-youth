Who We Are

Building Strong Islamic Character & Community Self-Reliance

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group is a registered community-based youth initiative centered in Changara, Teso North Sub-County, Busia County. Founded on the timeless principles of Islamic brotherhood, moral responsibility, and social welfare, our organization connects young Muslims to build a vibrant, empowered community.

From organizing our flagship Annual December Youth Seminars and Madrasa recitation challenges to carrying out Ramadan Iftar distribution and economic savings schemes, we aim to nurture righteous leaders who serve both the Ummah and the broader society in Kenya.

Islamic Values & Dawah

Strengthening faith, moral uprightness, and sound Islamic knowledge among youth.

Economic Empowerment

Fostering table-banking, youth entrepreneurship, and technical skill development.

Community Welfare

Providing food support during Ramadan & Eid, orphan care, and social assistance.

Inter-Mosque Unity

Uniting Muslim youth groups across all mosques in Teso North & Busia County.

Community Unity

Youth and children gathering at Changara for educational and welfare activities.

Community Impact

Core Activities & Community Programs

Delivering structured, meaningful initiatives that directly support youth development and community welfare in Busia County.

Ramadan & Eid Iftar Drives

Organizing community Iftar meals at local mosques, distributing food baskets to needy families, orphans, and elders throughout the holy month of Ramadan.

Annual Initiative

Eid al-Adha Qurbani Project

Facilitating livestock sacrifices and distributing Qurbani meat packages to vulnerable households in Changara and surrounding villages in Teso North.

Sacrifice & Distribution

Annual December Youth Seminar

Hosting our premier multi-day seminar bringing together youth delegations, scholars, and guest speakers for workshops on faith, ethics, and career building.

Flagship Event

Youth Savings & Enterprise

Operating a structured youth self-help group savings scheme (table banking) enabling young entrepreneurs to launch small businesses and farming projects.

Self-Reliance

Madrasa & Quranic Competitions

Supporting local madrasas with teaching materials, organizing annual Quran recitation and Hadith memorization contests with awards for students.

Educational Aid

Inter-Mosque Youth Network

Building active communication channels between mosque committees, youth representatives, and community leaders across Busia County.
