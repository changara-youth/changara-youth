Changara Jam'a
Sub-County Mosque Hub • Teso North

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group is a community-based organization rooted at Changara Jam'a Mosque (P.O. Box 50201), Teso North Sub-County, Busia County. We exist to unite young Muslims under authentic Islamic principles, self-reliance through agriculture, and active civic participation.

From our annual December Youth Seminars to local town sanitation cleanups, maize farming projects, table banking, and youth sports tournaments, we actively build unity and prosperity across local mosques in Teso North.

Islamic Values & Dawah

Grounded in Quranic teachings, moral formation, and mentorship.

Agricultural Enterprise

Communal maize farming and food crop production for self-reliance.

Town Sanitation & Cleanups

Regular market center and mosque sanitation campaigns.

Youth Sports & Unity

Football tournaments connecting youth across Teso North mosques.

Our Key Pillars

Core Initiatives

Transforming youth potential through practical community action, agriculture, sports, and religious education across Busia County.

Sanitation

Community Sanitation & Cleanups

Youth volunteers equipped with tools clean market centers, roadsides, and mosque compounds across Teso North.

Farming

Youth Agricultural Projects

Communal maize farming and crop cultivation providing food security and economic self-reliance for youth members.

Sports

Youth Sports & Football

Organizing soccer tournaments across mosque teams to foster physical fitness, teamwork, and healthy youth engagement.

Education

Madrasa & Quran Competitions

Supporting Islamic learning sessions, recitation contests, and academic mentorship for youth and children.

Table Banking & Micro-Loans

Structured youth savings groups providing interest-free micro-loans to start small businesses and farming enterprises.

Ramadan Iftar & Social Welfare

Organizing community food distribution and communal Iftar for families, orphans, and elders during holy months.

Interactive Tool

Youth Table Banking & Crop Impact Estimator

Estimate how modest monthly table banking savings or agricultural land contributions translate into harvested maize bags and business startup funds for young members in Teso North.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join Changara Muslim Youth?
Any young Muslim residing within Teso North Sub-County or Busia County interested in faith development, agricultural self-reliance, and community service is welcome to join.
Where is Changara Muslim Youth headquartered?
Our main operations hub is located at Changara Jam'a Mosque (P.O. Box 50201), Teso North Sub-County, Busia County, Kenya.
How can our mosque participate in the December 2026 Seminar?
You can register your delegation through the "Register Mosque Delegation" modal on this website or contact Chairman Abdulkarim Omoit directly via WhatsApp at +254 746 805529.
