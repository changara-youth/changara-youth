Who We Are

Fostering Faith, Unity & Economic Reliance in Teso North

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group (CMY) was formed to mobilize young Muslims in Changara, Teso North Sub-County of Busia County. Our primary focus is building a strong, self-reliant youth community grounded in Islamic values, agricultural entrepreneurship, and community service.

Islamic Knowledge

Hosting annual Quran recitation contests and youth islamic seminars across local mosques.

Youth Agri-Business

Commercial maize, passion fruit, and poultry farming to eliminate youth unemployment.

Table Banking & Micro-Loans

Providing interest-free or shariah-compliant micro-loans to empower youth businesses.

Community Welfare

Ramadan food distribution, environmental cleaning, and supporting orphans in Busia.

Our Mission

"To empower Muslim youth in Teso North through holistic spiritual growth, sustainable agricultural ventures, financial table banking, and benevolent community outreach."

Our Vision

"To become the leading model youth self-help organization in Western Kenya, recognized for moral integrity, youth enterprise, and exemplary Islamic leadership."

Core Values
Islamic Ethics Integrity & Transparency Youth Self-Reliance Community Brotherhood
Economic Empowerment

CMY Youth Agri-Business Initiatives

Agriculture is our main engine for youth employment in Teso North. We cultivate maize, passion fruit, vegetables, and manage poultry to create sustainable revenue for youth members.

Cereal Farming

Commercial Maize & Legumes

Utilizing leased and group-owned land in Changara to produce grain for local markets in Busia and Malaba border town.

Horticulture

Passion Fruit & Vegetables

High-yield passion fruit orchards generating weekly cash flow and providing youth members with practical farming skills.

Livestock

Poultry & Table Banking

Rearing improved Kienyeji chickens for eggs and meat, paired with CMY monthly table banking contributions.
