Who We Are

About Changara Muslim Youth

Founded in Changara Division, Teso North, the Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group (CMY) is a registered community-based self-help organization bringing together passionate young people committed to positive social and economic transformation.

We address key local challenges such as youth unemployment, environmental degradation, and financial exclusion through structured group savings (Table Banking), agricultural projects, skill training, and community service initiatives.

Our Motto:

"Pamoja Tuinuke (#Jiamini)" - Standing together for self-reliance and community growth.

Our Vision

To build a prosperous, united, and self-sustaining youth community in Teso North through ethical socio-economic initiatives.

Our Mission

To empower young people with practical agricultural skills, micro-finance opportunities, and moral leadership to foster sustainable growth.

Our Core Values

Unity

Fostering strong brotherhood and community ties

Empowerment

Creating economic opportunities for all members

Transparent financial and administrative practices

Service

Uplifting the vulnerable across Teso North

Key Pillars

Objectives & Strategic Programs

Focused interventions aimed at creating sustainable impact in Busia County.

1. Agriculture & Agribusiness

Promoting modern farming practices, poultry farming, horticulture, and crop diversification among youth to achieve food security and steady income.

2. Savings & Table Banking

Operating a transparent Table Banking model that enables members to access affordable micro-loans for personal development and business expansion.

3. Youth Empowerment & Skills

Organizing vocational workshops, entrepreneurship mentorship, digital literacy training, and career guidance for young community members.

4. Environmental Conservation

Leading tree planting drives in schools and public spaces across Changara, promoting clean energy and climate resilience awareness.

5. Community Welfare & Sadaqah

Supporting needy families, orphans, and elderly community members through emergency welfare funds, school fees assistance, and food drives.

6. Civic Engagement & Values

Encouraging moral uprightness, peaceful coexistence, anti-drug abuse campaigns, and active civic participation in local governance.

Get Involved

Membership & Welfare

Become an active member of Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group.

How to Join

1

Eligibility Criteria

Open to enthusiastic youth residing in or connected to Changara Division, Teso North, Busia County who are committed to group values.

2

Registration & Dues

Complete the membership interest form and pay the official group registration fee as outlined in the CMY Constitution.

3

Active Participation

Attend regular group meetings, contribute to weekly table banking savings, and participate in community service projects.

Key Member Benefits

  • Access to Micro-Credit: Affordable soft loans through our Table Banking program for business or emergency needs.
  • Agricultural Support: Shared training, seeds, and group labor support for farm projects.
  • Welfare Safety Net: Benevolent support during major life events (bereavement, weddings, medical emergencies).
  • Skills Development: Capacity building in leadership, financial management, and modern farming.
