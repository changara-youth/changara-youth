Our Core Vision & Community Goals

The Changara Muslim Youth Group operates at the heart of Changara, Teso North Sub-County. We foster an inclusive environment where children, young adults, and women can access educational mentorship, spiritual growth (Halaqa), agricultural training, and humanitarian support.

Islamic Knowledge

Daily Madrasa, weekend Quranic studies, Tajweed, and jurisprudence workshops for all ages.

Agriculture & Self-Reliance

Empowering youth through communal farming projects, maize cultivation, and sustainable agribusiness.

Sisters & Women Forum

Dedicated platforms for sisters' education, family rights (Haki ya Wazazi), and craft workshops.

Community Welfare

Charitable outreach, communal meals, medical support, and youth mentorship circles.

Empower a Youth, Transform a Community

Support our ongoing educational and infrastructure development projects in Changara.
