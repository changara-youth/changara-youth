Who We Are

Building a resilient, educated, and self-sufficient Muslim youth.

Changara Muslim Youth (CMY) is a dedicated self-help group centered in Changara, Teso North Sub-County. We unify youth from local mosques to promote sound Islamic moral values, foster economic independence, support vulnerable community members, and host transformative educational programs.

Islamic Values

Nurturing sound Aqeedah, moral conduct, and regular Quranic learning among youth.

Economic Power

Table-banking, enterprise development, and vocational mentorship for self-reliance.

Community Welfare

Orphan support, Ramadan food distribution, and emergency assistance in Teso North.

Inter-Mosque Unity

Connecting delegations across mosques for collaborative outreach and learning.
